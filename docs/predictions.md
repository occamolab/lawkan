# Pre-specified predictions log

This is the paper's reporting protocol: **every experiment pre-specified a prediction
in the authors' internal experiment log before its run, and refuted predictions are
kept and reported**. Each row is one pre-specified prediction with the outcome that was
measured and the verdict that follows. This file has 119 rows (the main battery, late
probes and the X22--X36 revision round); the paper's Appendix K further counts 11
plan-specified branch probes (X37--X61), summarized in its verdict table and entered
in the experiment plan. The machine-readable source is the paper's supplementary
predictions file (three longtables), from which this file is generated by
`tools/build_predictions_md.py`. Entries record the values measured at pre-specification
time; the current matched-floor correction (E40) gives a 2.733° floor and +0.05° for
LawKAN and supersedes the historical 2.697°/+0.09° quoted in some rows.
The X6b row records the pre-specified TOST check; the paper reports the final fits as
deterministic agreement, not a statistical-equivalence test. P-POOL is the historical
primary (58.3); the final POOL2 primary is 58.5 and the extended Fisher (with Kinova)
is 78.1. The internal log and its dated commits live in the private paper repository;
this public file lists prediction, outcome, and verdict.

**How to read a row**

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `#` | prediction ID — a stable label. In the main battery it is `P<n>` (late probes use `P-RC…`, `P-MK…`, `P-FR…`). In the revision round it is named after the experiment, so `X33-1` is the first prediction pre-specified for experiment `X33`. |
| `Expt` | experiment ID. Its script (where shipped in this repository) and its paper location are in [`blocks.md`](blocks.md) and the paper appendices; the pre-run entry and the verdict are in the paper's experiment plan. |
| Pre-specified prediction | what was written in the internal log *before* the run. |
| Outcome | the measured result. |
| Verdict | see below. |

**Verdicts:** ✓ the pre-specified threshold was met (or the pre-specified boundary branch
occurred); ◐ the predicted direction held but the threshold was not met (reported as
such, never silently upgraded); ✗ refuted (kept and reported); *inconclusive* /
*not triggered* a pre-specified non-signal or conditional branch. All p-values are
per-comparison.

**Scope.** Three parts: (1) the main battery (`P1–P63`); (2) the late
validity/physics probes (`P-RC…` through `P-FRIC`); (3) the revision round on the
capacity and locality of the law correction (`P-LB/P-FRG/P-B/P-FB/L/X32/P-FRK/X33`,
experiments X22–X36), in which two mechanism hypotheses were refuted. Referenced by
Appendix K of the paper.

An `Expt` is a link where the experiment ships in this repository. Four battery
probes — `E27c`, `E27d`, `E27e`, `E28` (the embedded-node component pilot) — are
retained in the log for completeness but have no public script (they are not cited
in the paper), so their cells stay plain text; the same holds for the three
non-experiment rows (withdrawn, `10-seed`, `cluster`).
